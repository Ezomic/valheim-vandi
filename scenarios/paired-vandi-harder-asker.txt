#! partner paired-vandi-harder-owner
#! nostage
# LHM-87, TWO CLIENTS ON A DEDICATED SERVER: the side that makes the offering and asks. The other half
# owns the altar, so everything the ask has to do crosses a machine. Neither proves anything alone.
# Needs VandiProbe on both clients and this character in the server's adminlist for `location`.
#
# The altar's owner is checked from both sides before any of it counts, because a world's altar goes to
# whoever is nearest and a run where it went to the asker would pass as singleplayer does.
#
# Round 1: this side asks, the owner spawns. A second offering during the delay is turned away by the
# owner, and must not wipe the ask: this is what a clear in InitiateSpawnBoss would break.
# Round 2: the next summon asks for nothing.
# Round 3: this side's ask goes stale, and the owner's own summon must not pay for it. Isolation: one
# player's forgotten ask does not harden another player's boss, or stamp it.
#
# Each client's removal travels as a message to the server, and every `until asked=none` is read on the
# client's local copy, so it only reads none once the server has accepted the removal and rebroadcast.

unkey rig_vh_altar
unkey rig_vh_owned
unkey rig_vh_r1_seen
unkey rig_vh_r2
unkey rig_vh_r2_seen
unkey rig_vh_r3
unkey rig_vh_r3_stale
unkey rig_vh_done
unkey vandi_harder_defeated_eikthyr
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
clear
god
ghost
wait 2

location Eikthyrnir
wait 5
banish Eikthyr 80
key vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 1
key rig_vh_altar
waitfor rig_vh_owned 300
# Until, because the rig key arrives before the altar's ZDO does: the first run read its own stale copy.
mod vandiprobe altar
until 20 altarowner=other

# Round 1.
mod vandiprobe arm 1
give TrophyDeer:4
wait 1
offer TrophyDeer
wait 1
keyis vandi_harder_defeated_eikthyr 1

# The owner's IsBossSpawnQueued is true and this machine's is not, so this reaches the owner and is refused.
offer TrophyDeer
wait 1
keyis vandi_harder_defeated_eikthyr 1
carrying TrophyDeer 2

appears Eikthyr 90 80
wait 3
level Eikthyr 3 80
mod vandiprobe boss Eikthyr
printed harder=yes stamp=1 level=3 summoned=yes mine=yes owner=other yes
mod vandiprobe asked defeated_eikthyr
until 15 asked=none
printed harderkeys=0 yes
key rig_vh_r1_seen

waitfor rig_vh_r2 120
kill Eikthyr 80
wait 4
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 2

# Round 2.
give TrophyDeer:2
wait 1
offer TrophyDeer
appears Eikthyr 90 80
wait 3
level Eikthyr 3 80
mod vandiprobe boss Eikthyr
printed harder=no stamp=0 level=3 summoned=yes mine=yes owner=other yes
key rig_vh_r2_seen

waitfor rig_vh_r3 120
banish Eikthyr 80
wait 2

# Round 3: an ask with no offering, left lying for the owner's summon.
mod vandiprobe ask defeated_eikthyr 2
wait 2
keyis vandi_harder_defeated_eikthyr 2
key rig_vh_r3_stale

waitfor rig_vh_done 200
mod vandiprobe asked defeated_eikthyr
until 15 asked=none
mod vandiprobe boss Eikthyr
printed harder=no stamp=0 level=1 summoned=yes mine=no yes

banish Eikthyr 300
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 2
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
