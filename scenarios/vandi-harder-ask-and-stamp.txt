#! nostage
# LHM-87: an accepted offering that asks for one star more gets it, stamped on the boss, and the next
# summon is an ordinary one.
#
# Needs the VandiProbe DLL in the profile (tools\VandiProbe), which calls VandiApi by reflection the
# way Hausi does. `arm` asks from a postfix on RPC_BossSpawnInitiated, Hausi's own seam, so the ask
# really does arrive inside the altar's call chain. In singleplayer that is inside InitiateSpawnBoss
# itself, which is what a clear placed in the wrong spot would eat.
#
# Why a pass means what it says: one kill on record is level 2, so level 3 can only come from the ask.
# The second summon has two kills and is level 3 by the record alone, so the same level with harder=no
# and the same maxhp shows the stamp is what differs and the health multiplier treats them alike.
clear
god
ghost

unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
unkey vandi_harder_defeated_eikthyr
key vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 1
wait 1

location Eikthyrnir
wait 3
banish Eikthyr 80

mod vandiprobe arm 1
give TrophyDeer:2
wait 1
offer TrophyDeer

# Published between the acceptance and the spawn, with the stars as its value.
wait 1
keyis vandi_harder_defeated_eikthyr 1

appears Eikthyr 90 80
wait 2
level Eikthyr 3 80
mod vandiprobe boss Eikthyr
printed harder=yes stamp=1 level=3 summoned=yes mine=yes yes
note hp maxhp=([0-9]+)
logged 1 asked for yes

# Read and removed, so it speaks for one summon.
mod vandiprobe asked defeated_eikthyr
until 10 asked=none
printed harderkeys=0 yes

# Credit is the summoner's as for any other boss, and the record gains one kill, not one per star.
kill Eikthyr 80
wait 3
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 2

# The next summon asks for nothing, and gets nothing more than two kills earn.
give TrophyDeer:2
wait 1
offer TrophyDeer
appears Eikthyr 90 80
wait 2
level Eikthyr 3 80
mod vandiprobe boss Eikthyr
printed harder=no stamp=0 level=3 summoned=yes yes
went hp maxhp=([0-9]+) same

banish Eikthyr 300
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
