# Vandi - the altar, the stars, and who the kill belongs to.
#
# It brings its own altar. `location Eikthyrnest` spawns a real Eikthyr altar in front of you
# through the game's own test-spawn call, and it is gone on the next load, so stand anywhere
# with a little open ground ahead - the Meadows is simplest.
#
# Everything here needs the real summoning path, because that is what the mod hooks: the
# offering says who made it, the altar's owner reads that when the boss appears, and the kill is
# credited off the stamp on the creature. Spawning Eikthyr with the scenario's own spawn step
# would test the spawn step. So the trophies go in through `offer`, which is
# OfferingBowl.UseItem - the call the hotbar makes, and the one that reaches InitiateSpawnBoss.
#
# The first draft of this file used `goto` and `use` on the altar, failed at step 6 on its first
# run on 2026-09-24, and could never have passed: both steps search building pieces, an altar is
# part of a location and not a piece, and pressing E on this kind of altar does nothing anyway.
#
# What it proves, in order: a boss summoned with nothing on the record arrives plain, the kill
# is credited to the summoner, and the next one arrives with a star because of it.

clear
god
ghost

# A clean record for this character. The scenario owns this key for the length of the run.
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
wait 1

location Eikthyrnest
wait 3
give TrophyDeer:2
wait 1

# The offering. Five seconds is the altar's own spawn delay; the wait is longer to leave room
# for the effects and for the stamp to be written by whoever owns the altar.
offer TrophyDeer
wait 9

# Never killed him, so he is exactly the boss the game makes.
level Eikthyr 1

kill Eikthyr
wait 3

# The credit. This is the whole point of the mod: it went to the character that paid for the
# offering, and it is one, not two.
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 1

# And again, which is where the record starts to cost something. One previous kill is one
# star, and one star is level 2.
give TrophyDeer:2
wait 1
offer TrophyDeer
wait 9
level Eikthyr 2

kill Eikthyr
wait 3
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 2

unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
