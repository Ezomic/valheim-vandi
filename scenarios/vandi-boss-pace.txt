# LHM-84: a summoned boss moves and swings 5 percent faster per star, none at zero stars.
#
# Run with Vandi Verbose = true and Utangard BlockBossSummons = false. The kills are seeded with
# `key`, not fought. The three sections summon Bonemass at 0, 1 and 2 stars in that order, and the
# 0 star section comes first because it asserts that no pace line has been written yet: `logged`
# reads everything since the run began, so a later section would see the earlier ones.
#
# What this proves is the VALUES the boss ran on, from the Verbose log the component writes when it
# applies them and when an attack starts. The log is the only evidence of the speeds: nothing here
# measures metres a second, and nothing here SEES the faster swing.
#
# MANUAL CHECKS, which this cannot do:
#  1. Watch a two star Bonemass walk and swing. Want: a little brisker than a plain one, and no
#     frozen frames or snapping at the start or end of a swing.
#  2. Look for a swing that plays at the wrong speed AFTER it ends. Want: none, the walk is normal.
clear
god

unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_bonemass
wait 1

location Bonemass
wait 3

banish Bonemass 80
give WitheredBone:10
wait 1

offer WitheredBone
wait 0.5
appears Bonemass 40 100
level Bonemass 1 100
wait 3

logged Boss pace: Bonemass at 0 star(s), left alone yes
logged Boss pace: Bonemass at 1 star(s) no
logged Boss pace: Bonemass at 2 star(s) no
logged Boss pace: Bonemass swings no

banish Bonemass 80
wait 2

key vandi_p_$player_defeated_bonemass 1
wait 1

give WitheredBone:10
wait 1

offer WitheredBone
wait 0.5
appears Bonemass 40 100
level Bonemass 2 100
wait 3

logged Boss pace: Bonemass at 1 star(s), move x1.050, attack x1.050. yes

banish Bonemass 80
wait 2

unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_bonemass
key vandi_p_$player_defeated_bonemass 2
wait 1

give WitheredBone:10
wait 1

offer WitheredBone
wait 0.5
appears Bonemass 40 100
level Bonemass 3 100
wait 3

logged Boss pace: Bonemass at 2 star(s), move x1.100, attack x1.100. yes

# The boss walks to the player and swings. 25 seconds is generous; the log line is written on the
# first frame of each swing the pace changes.
wait 25
logged swings at animator speed 1.100 yes

unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_bonemass
