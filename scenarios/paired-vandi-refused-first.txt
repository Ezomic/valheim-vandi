#! partner paired-vandi-refused-second
#! nostage
# LHM-88, TWO CLIENTS ON A DEDICATED SERVER: the player whose offering the altar accepts. A second
# player's offering, refused during the spawn delay, must change nothing about whose boss this is.
# Needs VandiProbe on both clients and this character in the server's adminlist for `location`.
#
# Round 1 is the one that fails before the fix, and it has to be this shape. The refused offering
# only reaches InitiateSpawnBoss on a client that does not own the altar, because UseItem's own
# IsBossSpawnQueued check turns the owner away first, and only the owner's answers true. So with two
# clients this side owns the altar and makes the first offering, and the other side offers second.
# The two records differ so the level says whose it was: 1 kill here and none there, plus 1 star asked
# for here, makes level 3 for this player and level 2 for the other.
#
# Round 2 hands the altar to the other side, so the note has to cross a machine inside the delay. That
# side offers during the delay as well, and is turned away locally, so it never touches the note. Before
# the fix this round still fails its numbers, but only because round 1 credited the wrong record.
#
# Round 3: the next accepted summon is the other player's, at the other player's record. A note that
# stuck to the first player would fail here.

unkey rig_vr_altar
unkey rig_vr_ready1
unkey rig_vr_r1_offered
unkey rig_vr_r1_second
unkey rig_vr_r1_seen
unkey rig_vr_r1_seen_b
unkey rig_vr_r1_killed
unkey rig_vr_r2_owned
unkey rig_vr_r2_ready
unkey rig_vr_r2_second
unkey rig_vr_r2_seen
unkey rig_vr_r2_killed
unkey rig_vr_r3_seen
unkey rig_vr_done
unkey vandi_harder_defeated_eikthyr
unkey vandi_summon_defeated_eikthyr
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
clear
god
ghost
wait 2

location Eikthyrnir
wait 5
banish Eikthyr 80
key vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 1
key rig_vr_altar
waitfor rig_vr_ready1 300

# Round 1: this machine owns the altar and offers first. Checked from both sides before it counts.
mod vandiprobe claimaltar
printed altarowner=me yes
key rig_vr_r1_owned
waitfor rig_vr_r1_owned_b 60

mod vandiprobe arm 1
give TrophyDeer:4
wait 1
offer TrophyDeer
wait 1
key rig_vr_r1_offered
waitfor rig_vr_r1_second 30

appears Eikthyr 90 80
wait 3
level Eikthyr 3 80
mod vandiprobe boss Eikthyr
printed harder=yes stamp=1 level=3 summoned=yes mine=yes owner=me yes
key rig_vr_r1_seen
waitfor rig_vr_r1_seen_b 60

kill Eikthyr 80
wait 4
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 2
key rig_vr_r1_killed

# Round 2: the other machine owns the altar. The note is cleared so a pass cannot be round 1's.
waitfor rig_vr_r2_owned 120
unkey vandi_summon_defeated_eikthyr
mod vandiprobe altar
until 20 altarowner=other
wait 2
offer TrophyDeer
wait 1
key rig_vr_r2_ready
waitfor rig_vr_r2_second 30

appears Eikthyr 90 80
wait 3
level Eikthyr 3 80
mod vandiprobe boss Eikthyr
printed harder=no stamp=0 level=3 summoned=yes mine=yes owner=other yes
key rig_vr_r2_seen
waitfor rig_vr_r2_seen_b 60

kill Eikthyr 80
wait 4
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 3
key rig_vr_r2_killed

# Round 3: the other player's own summon, and nothing of it lands on this record.
waitfor rig_vr_r3_seen 120
mod vandiprobe boss Eikthyr
printed harder=no stamp=0 level=1 summoned=yes mine=no owner=other yes
key rig_vr_r3_seen_a
waitfor rig_vr_done 120
wait 3
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 3

banish Eikthyr 300
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
