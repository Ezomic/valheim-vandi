# Vandi - the compendium page shows what Malmr has tied to a boss. LHM-45.
#
# What it proves. With Malmr installed, the page says for the Black Forest boss what its copper
# veins are waiting for, in the order a player meets it: two more kills while none is on the record,
# the next kill after one, "open" at the second, and "waits for Pickaxes" when the kill is done and
# the skill is not. It also lists, under "The road ahead", the other bosses whose metals are still
# waiting, with the wording the mockup has. Every one of those lines comes from Malmr's own gate
# through MalmrLink, so this is the file that notices Malmr moving a member the page reads.
#
# The kills are seeded with `key` rather than fought. This is a test of what the page draws.
# vandi-compendium-page does the real kill through the altar, and Malmr's own scenarios test its gate.
#
# What it needs. Malmr in the profile at its defaults: Unlocks Copper:30 and Silver:50 and Eitr:60,
# Bosses Copper on defeated_gdking, Silver on defeated_dragon and Eitr on defeated_queen, BossKills 2,
# and Vandi installed so the count is Vandi's. The game in English. Without Malmr the Black Forest
# page has no Malmr box at all, which is correct and which this file would report as a failure on
# its first panel step, so run it only where Malmr is.
#
# What it leaves behind. The Pickaxes skill is put back with `skill Pickaxes back`, and the three
# records are removed.

clear
god
ghost

skill Pickaxes 35
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_gdking
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_dragon
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_queen
wait 1

compendium
wait 1
press Vandi
wait 1
press The Elder
wait 1

# No kill yet: two to go, and the level is already met.
panel Malmr, tied to this boss yes
panel 2 more kills open Copper vein mining yes
panel Needs a one-star kill of The Elder, your second, and Pickaxes 30 yes
panel The road ahead yes
panel Moder: never killed, first kill opens nothing yet, the second opens Silver yes
panel The Queen: never killed, first kill opens nothing yet, the second opens Giant brains yes
tall Malmr, tied to this boss
tall The road ahead
tall 2 more kills open Copper vein mining

# One kill: the next one opens it.
key vandi_p_$player_defeated_gdking 1
wait 2
panel The next kill opens Copper vein mining yes

# Two kills, the one-star kill: open, and the line says what it took.
key vandi_p_$player_defeated_gdking 2
wait 2
panel Copper vein mining is open yes
panel Needed Pickaxes 30 and a one-star kill of The Elder, your second yes
tall Copper vein mining is open

# The kill is done and the skill is not.
skill Pickaxes 20
wait 2
panel Copper vein mining waits for Pickaxes 30 yes
panel You are at Pickaxes 20 yes

# Moder, once killed, is one more kill from silver, and the line carries the skill it also needs.
key vandi_p_$player_defeated_dragon 1
wait 2
panel Moder: one more kill opens Silver (Pickaxes 50) yes
tall The road ahead

# A boss Malmr ties nothing to has no Malmr box. Yagluth owns the Plains and no entry waits for him.
press Yagluth
wait 1
panel Malmr, tied to this boss no
close

skill Pickaxes back
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_gdking
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_dragon
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_queen
