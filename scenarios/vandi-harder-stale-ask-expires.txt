#! nostage
# LHM-87: an ask whose boss never came does not make the next plain summon harder.
#
# The ask is made with no offering at all, which is the state an altar owner leaving during the spawn
# delay leaves behind: a key in the world and no boss coming. Needs VandiProbe.
#
# Why a pass means what it says: `keyis` proves the stale key is really there first, so level 1 and
# harder=no are the expiry at work and not an ask that was refused. The last section is a fresh ask on
# top of a stale one with different stars, and its value decides the level, so it cannot pass by
# clearing everything: the clear runs first and the fresh ask survives it. A clear in the wrong place
# fails this section in singleplayer, where the ask lands inside InitiateSpawnBoss.
clear
god
ghost

unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
unkey vandi_harder_defeated_eikthyr
wait 1

location Eikthyrnir
wait 3
banish Eikthyr 80

mod vandiprobe ask defeated_eikthyr 2
printed ask=called yes
wait 1
keyis vandi_harder_defeated_eikthyr 2

give TrophyDeer:2
wait 1
offer TrophyDeer

# Gone at the acceptance, not at the spawn.
wait 1
mod vandiprobe asked defeated_eikthyr
printed asked=none yes

appears Eikthyr 90 80
wait 2
level Eikthyr 1 80
mod vandiprobe boss Eikthyr
printed harder=no stamp=0 level=1 summoned=yes yes
banish Eikthyr 80
wait 2

# Stale at two stars, fresh at one: the boss is one star harder.
mod vandiprobe ask defeated_eikthyr 2
wait 1
keyis vandi_harder_defeated_eikthyr 2
mod vandiprobe arm 1
give TrophyDeer:2
wait 1
offer TrophyDeer
wait 1
keyis vandi_harder_defeated_eikthyr 1
appears Eikthyr 90 80
wait 2
level Eikthyr 2 80
mod vandiprobe boss Eikthyr
printed harder=yes stamp=1 level=2 yes

banish Eikthyr 300
