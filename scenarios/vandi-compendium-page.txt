#! nostage
# Vandi - the compendium page shows your kills and moves with them. LHM-45.
#
# What it proves. The compendium has a Vandi page, drawn as a panel: a list of bosses by biome on
# the left and the one you pick on the right. With no kill on the record the Meadows boss reads
# "not killed yet", +0% starred creatures and a boss that comes back with no stars. After a real
# kill, made through the altar so the credit is the summoner's, the same page reads one kill, +5%,
# and one star on the next summon. And it keeps up while it is open: the record is moved to three
# kills with the page showing and the numbers follow within its one-second refresh, ending at the
# star cap with "The cap, so it stays 2".
#
# Every figure on the page is the figure the world uses: Kills.LocalCount, Stars.Earned and
# Summon.StarsFor, the same functions the star roll and the altar call. So this file also holds the
# page to the keyis check on the record the kill wrote. The page is read through the labels it
# draws, never through Vandi's own code.
#
# The `tall` steps are the guard that matters most on this page. On 2026-09-29 Utangard's panel,
# built the same way, drew every text at no height on top of the others after the compendium's text
# was blanked, and every string was still present, so a `panel` step passed over it. `tall` measures
# the label against the text it holds. They run on the first look and again after a refresh, since
# the first draw is a different frame from the one that went wrong.
#
# It brings its own altar, like vandi-summoner-gets-the-credit, and for the same reasons: NO STAGE,
# stand anywhere with a little open ground ahead, the Meadows is simplest, and start it from
# somewhere nothing has levelled when the altar's ground matters.
#
# What it needs. A world you host, the game in English (the boss and biome names are clicked and
# read by their English names), ShowCompendiumPage on, and Vandi's default StarChancePerKill 5,
# StarChanceCap 25, BossStarCap 2 and HarderBosses on. The page opens on the Meadows boss because the
# first `location` leaves you standing in the Meadows; Eikthyr is clicked either way.
#
# What it leaves behind. The record for Eikthyr is removed at the end, as vandi-stars-per-biome does.
# Nothing else outlives the run: the altar goes on the next load, and the kill is the one Eikthyr
# this run summoned.

clear
god
ghost

unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
wait 1

location Eikthyrnir
wait 3

# No Eikthyr but the one this run summons, for the reason vandi-summoner-gets-the-credit gives.
banish Eikthyr 80

# The page before any kill.
compendium
wait 1
press Vandi
wait 1
press Eikthyr
wait 1
panel The Meadows, Eikthyr yes
panel You have not killed Eikthyr yet yes
beside Starred creatures now | +0% yes
beside Next summon comes back at | no stars yes
beside Next summon comes back at | The next kill makes it 1 star yes
panel Kill 1 yes
panel Kill 5 yes
panel Kill 6 no

tall The Meadows, Eikthyr
tall Meadows
tall Eikthyr
tall Starred creatures now
tall Next summon comes back at
tall Kill 1
tall Boss, by biome (your kills)
close

# A real kill, through the altar: the offering says who paid, and the corpse credits them.
give TrophyDeer:2
wait 1
offer TrophyDeer
wait 20
kill Eikthyr
wait 3
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 1

# The same page, a kill later.
compendium
wait 1
press Vandi
wait 1
press Eikthyr
wait 1
panel You have killed Eikthyr 1 time. yes
beside Starred creatures now | +5% yes
beside Starred creatures now | Next kill: +10% yes
beside Next summon comes back at | 1 star yes
beside Next summon comes back at | The next kill makes it 2 stars yes

tall The Meadows, Eikthyr
tall Starred creatures now
tall Next summon comes back at
tall Kill 1

# Moved while the page stays open: three kills is +15%, and one more is the cap on the boss.
key vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 3
wait 2
panel You have killed Eikthyr 3 times. yes
beside Starred creatures now | +15% yes
beside Starred creatures now | Next kill: +20% yes
beside Next summon comes back at | 2 stars yes
beside Next summon comes back at | The cap, so it stays 2 yes

tall The Meadows, Eikthyr
tall Starred creatures now
tall Next summon comes back at

# Five kills is the cap on the star chance too, and nothing more can be added.
key vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr 5
wait 2
beside Starred creatures now | +25% yes
beside Starred creatures now | Already the most it gives yes
close

unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
