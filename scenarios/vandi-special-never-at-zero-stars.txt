#! nostage
# LHM-85: a boss with no stars never uses a special move, however long the fight runs.
#
# No kill is seeded, so Eikthyr arrives plain (level 1). With the Eikthyr charge's FirstDelay at 5
# seconds a starred one would start by now; this one waits twenty-odd seconds and the log must show
# only "waiting, level 1" and never "starting". Needs Vandi Verbose = true and
# EikthyrCharge FirstDelay = 5, as vandi-special-charge does.
clear
god

unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_eikthyr
wait 1

location Eikthyrnir
wait 3

banish Eikthyr 80
give TrophyDeer:2
wait 1
offer TrophyDeer
wait 0.5
appears Eikthyr 40 100
level Eikthyr 1 100

wait 25
screenshot

logged Boss specials: waiting, level 1 yes
logged Boss special charge: starting no
logged Boss special charge: warning sent no

banish Eikthyr 300
